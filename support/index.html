Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with a purchase

Email tuomas.peronvuo@gmail.com and include your iPhone or iPad model and the app version (Settings › About in the app). We usually reply within a few days.

Purchases

  • Restore Plus on a new device: Settings › Plus › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel a subscription: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Mushro, or Settings › Plus › Manage subscription in the app.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Safety

Mushro’s photo results are suggestions, never confirmed identifications. Never eat a wild mushroom on the strength of an app. If someone may have eaten a poisonous mushroom, call your local poison centre or emergency number now — in Finland, Myrkytystietokeskus 0800 147 111 or 112 in an emergency.
