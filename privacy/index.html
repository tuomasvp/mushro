Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Suomeksi

In short: Mushro collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app — your photos, notes, favourites and saved places — stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Mushro is published by Tuomas Peronvuo, a private individual in Finland (“we”). Contact: tuomas.peronvuo@gmail.com.

Photo identification happens on your device

When you photograph a mushroom, the photos are analysed by a model built into the app, on your iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded, and we never receive them. Photos you take for identification are kept only while you use that screen, unless you save them to your fieldbook.

Your fieldbook

Favourites, field records, notes, dates and any locations you add are stored only on your device, and in your device backups depending on your iOS settings. We cannot see them. You can export, import and delete them in the app; deleting the app deletes them.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while it is open — for example to suggest your region or to pin a find in your fieldbook. Your location is processed on the device and is never sent to us. Maps in the app are provided by Apple Maps under Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Mushro Plus is sold through the App Store. Payment and your Apple Account are handled by Apple; the app checks your purchase with Apple on the device. We receive only Apple’s anonymous sales reports, never your name, email or payment details. The number of free photo suggestions you have used this month is counted on your device.

Downloads and diagnostics

The field photographs are downloaded from the App Store after installation, as part of the app. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage reports; you can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data.
  • No profiling.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us with any privacy question at tuomas.peronvuo@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority; in Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Children

Mushro is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
